How to read and reproduce the counts

Each primary frequency is a count of distinct paragraphs containing a term: explicitly by lexical matching, or implicitly by a curated annotation. A term-paragraph pair cannot be both. Different terms can occur in the same paragraph, so totals across terms can exceed twelve.

Matching ignores case, recognizes listed aliases, uses word boundaries, and gives the longest overlapping phrase precedence. “Prompt injection” is therefore not also a plain “prompt” hit. Explicit mentions count accepted textual matches; “large language model (LLM)” has two mentions but one explicit paragraph.

Only the twelve anchored analysis paragraphs are counted. Titles, source tables, implementation lists, the vocabulary, and conclusions are excluded. Implicit counts reflect recorded interpretations with exact quotations and reasons; they are not an automatic or exhaustive measure of meaning. Learner review is pending.
